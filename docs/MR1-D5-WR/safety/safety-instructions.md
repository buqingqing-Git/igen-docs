# Safety Instructions

- All installation work must be carried out by well-trained professionals, and local laws and regulations must be complied with.
- The power must be completely disconnected and verified de-energized before installation and wiring. Live work is strictly prohibited.
- Do not open the product enclosure or connected devices.
- Stop using the device immediately if it is damaged. Within the warranty period, contact the manufacturer for repair or replacement; do not continue using it or attempt self-repair.
- Only use the device within the specified voltage range, see [Parameter Comparison](../parameter-comparison.md).
- Do not place the device in high-temperature environments or near open flames, and do not immerse it in any liquid.
- Keep the device out of the reach of children.
