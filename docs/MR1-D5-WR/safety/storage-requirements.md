# Storage Requirements

If the meter is not put into use immediately, store it as follows:

- Keep the outer packaging and inspect it every 3 months. Replace the packaging promptly if pest damage or packaging damage is found. If a device has been unpacked and is not used for the time being, put it back into the original packaging and seal it with tape.
- The storage temperature is −20 °C to +70 °C, and the relative humidity must not exceed 40% RH with no condensation. The ambient air must not contain corrosive or flammable gases.
- Store in a clean and dry place to prevent dust and moisture from corroding the device, and keep rain or standing water away.
- After two years of storage, the device must be inspected and tested by professionals before being put into use.
