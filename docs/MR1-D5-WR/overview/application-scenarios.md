# Application Scenarios

The D5 series is a micro smart meter that uses split-core current transformers (CT). It measures voltage, current, active power, power factor, frequency, active energy and other parameters in real time, and sends the data to a local or remote software platform over Wi-Fi for monitoring and analysis of energy usage.

The product is suitable for bidirectional metering scenarios in European residential and small commercial and industrial applications, including:

- Remote 24-hour real-time monitoring of energy data.
- Device linkage through the local API, for integration into a home energy management system (HEMS).
- Long-distance data relay via LoRa-enabled models.
